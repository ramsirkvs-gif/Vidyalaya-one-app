# Consolidated survey presentation

In **Principal login → Task Manager**, save task edits and comments, then choose **Prepare presentation report** beside the task heading. This reads the latest saved records and opens a private preview. It does not publish survey answers.

The title is **Status, Causes of Low Performance of Students of PM SHRI KV No. 1 Itanagar (Survey Report from Vidyalaya)**. The A4 landscape main report, up to four pages, has Part I for the selected Class IX sections and Part II for the selected Classes IX–XII. The published questionnaire and figures are used when available; otherwise the saved private draft is labelled explicitly. Classes outside IX–XII are excluded.

Every numeric question, including Q1–Q3 class figures, has a pie chart or an explicit no-data/zero state. Student percentages use only classes with both that question's compatible answer and supplied enrolment. Missing enrolment switches the chart to a labelled class/grade distribution. PTMs show meeting distributions, never student percentages. Blank answers are excluded rather than treated as zero; answers to changed questions are excluded until confirmed under the current wording. Categories overlap and must not be added into a unique student count. The survey records reported circumstances, not proof of causation.

Written questionnaire answers and overall teacher comments follow in **Appendix A**, outside the four main pages. The Principal's saved review is included there. The final main page lists actual submitting teachers/prime mentors in small print. Enter verified co-opted participants (Counsellor, Special Educator, Yoga Teacher, or others) in the preview and choose **Update participant list** before printing. These additional names are preview-only and are not saved to the task.

Choose **Print / Save PDF**, use the supplied A4 landscape page size, and disable browser-added headers/footers. Print all pages to include comments, or only the numbered main-report pages (two or four, according to your question selection) for the presentation alone. Use **Select presentation questions** to check only the points needed in both sections. Original question numbers are retained. The presentation supports up to 24 selected points, including any selected Q1–Q3 figures; longer questionnaires can remain intact while you choose a subset for the presentation. Selecting up to 12 points produces two main pages; 13–24 points produce four. The full comments appendix is retained. These selections affect only the preview, not the task or submissions. Closing the preview or ending the Principal session clears it from the page. No report or comments are stored in browser storage or uploaded by the preview.

# Principal corrections

Submitted classes display a green **✓ Submitted** label; pending classes display **Pending** in red. In **Submission status and results**, choose **Open report**, then **Edit submitted answers — class**. Edit the submitted questionnaire answers or teacher remarks and choose **Save submitted answers**. Principal authentication authorizes this action without a teacher PIN. Q1–Q3 remain read-only; update those through the shared class-figures editor.

Corrections retain the original submitting teacher and submitted question wording. They update totals and subsequent presentations, retain a private previous-answer history, and show a confirmed Principal-save message. Changes to the same report reject stale saves instead of overwriting a newer submission. Teacher PIN permissions and public visibility remain unchanged.
